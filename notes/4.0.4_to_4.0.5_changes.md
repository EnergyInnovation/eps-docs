## 1. Three-component shadow cost for EV passenger LDVs (was two) 🟢

**What changed.** In 4.0.4, the price seen by EV-LDV-passenger purchasers added two shadow costs to the calculated vehicle price:

```
New Vehicle Price Including EV Shadow Costs[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
  = New Vehicle Price[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
  + Range Anxiety Shadow Cost
  + Charging Availability Shadow Cost
```

In 4.0.5, a third shadow cost is added:

```
New Vehicle Price Including EV Shadow Costs[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
  = New Vehicle Price[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
  + Range Anxiety Shadow Cost
  + Charging Availability Shadow Cost
  + Consumer Preference Shadow Cost[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
```

`Consumer Preference Shadow Cost` decays as the BEV share of the LDV passenger fleet grows:
```
Consumer Preference Shadow Cost[LDVs, passenger, BEV]
  = BCPSC BAU Consumer Preference Shadow Cost
    × (1 − Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology[LDVs, passenger, BEV])
```

`BCPSC` is read from `InputData/trans/BCPSC/BCPSC.csv` (single scalar input, units: `$/vehicle`). `Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology` is computed from a new level variable `Last Year Vehicles` (parallel structure to the existing `Vehicles` level).

Both `Range Anxiety Shadow Cost` and `Charging Availability Shadow Cost` equation bodies are **byte-identical to 4.0.4** (verified). They are not removed.

**Where it lives.** `Transportation - Main` view (and BAU mirror).

**Docs affected.**
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — section "Range Anxiety and Charging Time Shadow Cost" (just before "Calculating Number of New Vehicles"; image `RngAnxiety`). Section needs a rewrite to introduce the third component, explain the BEV-share decay mechanism, and update the prose that calls this "the third component" (in 4.0.5 it's the *fourth* if you count `New Vehicle Price` itself, or there are now three shadow costs instead of two).
- `reduce-range-anxiety-charging-time.md` — concept page. May or may not need an update; it currently describes the *barrier*, not the model machinery. Probably leave alone unless staff wants a reference to the new mechanism.
- `transportation-sector-cash.md` — explicit note in 4.0.4 main doc says shadow costs are *not* in cash flow tracking. That should remain true in 4.0.5 (verified — the new vars are not used in the cash view's equations).

## 2. Explicit EV charging-infrastructure cost layer 🟢

**What changed.** 4.0.5 adds machinery to model the *real cost* (not just shadow cost) of EV charging infrastructure for LDV passenger BEVs, threaded into the fuel-cost calculation:

New input data variables (all from CSVs under `InputData/trans/`):
- `EVCIC EV Charger Installation Costs` — `$/vehicle`, scalar
- `RoPCCtPCC Ratio of Public Charging Costs to Private Charging Costs` — `Dmnl`, scalar
- `SoVCaPC Share of Vehicle Charging at Private Chargers for Passenger LDVs` — lookup over BEV market share

New computed variables:
- `Share of Vehicle Charging at Private Chargers[Cargo Type, Vehicle Type]` — for `passenger, LDVs`, this is `LOOKUP INVERT(SoVCaPC, Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology[LDVs, passenger, BEV])`. For all other vehicle/cargo combinations, the BAU value is passed through (so non-LDV-passenger combinations see no change).
- `Share of New Passenger LDV EV Purchases Requiring Home Charger Installations = (1 − Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology[LDVs, passenger, BEV])` — i.e., the assumption is that as BEV share grows, fewer new buyers need to install a home charger because the existing stock of installed chargers carries over.
- `Added Cost for Passenger LDV EV EVSE Equipment[LDVs, passenger, BEV] = EVCIC × Share of New Passenger LDV EV Purchases Requiring Home Charger Installations`. Zero for all other Vehicle/Cargo/Tech combinations.

Threaded into the fuel-cost flow (described in §4 below):
```
Electricity Fuel Cost per Unit Energy by Vehicle and Cargo Type[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]
  = Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy[electricity tf]
       × Share of Vehicle Charging at Private Chargers[Cargo Type, Vehicle Type]
   + Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy[electricity tf]
       × (1 − Share of Vehicle Charging at Private Chargers[...])
       × RoPCCtPCC
```

**Where it lives.** `Transportation - Main` view (and BAU mirror), with a small footprint in `Transportation - Cash Flow` (subscript-expanded fuel-cost variables flow through).

**Docs affected.**
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — likely needs a new subsection (or a substantial extension to "New Vehicle Price") describing the EVSE installation cost, and a related update to the cargo-distance fuel-cost description (see §4) to explain the public/private charging mix.
- `ev-charger-deployment.md` — concept page; may benefit from a note acknowledging the 4.0.5 model now represents charger costs explicitly. **Confirm with staff whether this prose-only page should reference the new machinery.**
- `transportation-sector-cash.md` — has an existing "Change in Amount Spent on EV Chargers" subsection that uses `Number of EV Chargers Deployed by Policy × cost per charger`. **Phase 2 found no equation-level removal of that flow, so the existing section can stand. The new EVSE equipment cost is layered into vehicle prices, not into the existing charger-deployment cash flow.** This may warrant a brief sentence clarifying the two paths.

## 3. Battery cost is now disaggregated by Vehicle Type 🟢

**What changed.** In 4.0.4, `This Year Battery Portion of Vehicle Price` was:
```
This Year Battery Portion of Vehicle Price[Cargo Type, Vehicle Type, Vehicle Technology]
  = This Year Battery Capacity by Vehicle Type[...]
    × This Year Battery Manufacturing Price After Subsidies     (scalar)
```

In 4.0.5:
```
This Year Battery Portion of Vehicle Price[Cargo Type, Vehicle Type, Vehicle Technology]
  = This Year Battery Capacity by Vehicle Type[...]
    × This Year Battery Manufacturing Price After Subsidies[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]
    × BPM Battery Pack Markup[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]
```

Two additions:
1. `This Year Battery Manufacturing Price After Subsidies` is now subscripted by `[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]` (was scalar).
2. A new multiplier `BPM Battery Pack Markup[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]` is applied — read from `InputData/trans/BPM/BPM.csv`. New input.

Related new input:
- `BPPM Battery Pack Price Multiplier[Vehicle Type, passenger/freight]` — appears to feed the upstream battery manufacturing price calculation (`This Year Battery Manufacturing Price Before Subsidies by Vehicle Type = This Year Battery Manufacturing Price Before Subsidies × BPPM[...]`). Read from `InputData/trans/BPPM/BPPM-{psgr,frgt}.csv`.

In the cash flow view:
- `This Year Battery Manufacturing Price Before Subsidies` (scalar) and `BAU This Year Battery Manufacturing Price Before Subsidies` (scalar) were *removed from the sketch* (the equation-section variable still exists, but the cash view now references the `... by Vehicle Type` versions).
- New: `This Year Battery Subsidy Passed Through to Consumers by Vehicle Type[Cargo Type, Vehicle Type, Vehicle Technology]`. Used in the cash flow split.

**Where it lives.** Main view (battery price calc), Cash Flow view (battery subsidy disaggregation), BAU mirror.

**Docs affected.**
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — "New Vehicle Price" subsection. Specifically the paragraphs around images `BatteryCost`, `EndogenousEVLearning`, and `BatterySize`. Need to introduce `BPM` and `BPPM`, and update the description from "average battery pack price (in $/kWh)" to reflect that battery pricing now varies by vehicle type.
- `transportation-sector-cash.md` — "Change in Vehicle Battery Subsidy Payments" subsection. The doc says "we multiply the battery subsidy values by the number of vehicles sold and the battery capacity of each vehicle" — this remains true mechanically; the change is just that the per-vehicle-type breakout is now explicit.
- `endogenous-learning.md` — likely unaffected mechanically (the learning curve drives the pre-markup, pre-multiplier price), but the page should be reviewed in its own Phase 1/2 pass.

## 4. Fuel cost per unit energy is now subscripted by Vehicle and Cargo Type 🟢 (subscript expansion — flagged per CLAUDE.md)

**What changed.** 4.0.4 used `Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy[Transportation Fuel]` and `BAU Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy[Transportation Fuel]` as the single source of fuel cost. These variables still exist in 4.0.5 (unchanged equations).

4.0.5 *adds* two new variables that wrap them:
- `Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy by Vehicle and Cargo Type[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type, Transportation Fuel]`
- `BAU Transportation Fuel Cost per Unit Energy by Vehicle and Cargo Type[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type, Transportation Fuel]`

Behavior:
- For **non-electricity** fuels (`:EXCEPT: [Vehicle Type, Cargo Type, electricity tf]`), these pass through the underlying fuel-only-subscripted variable. No behavior change for non-electricity fuels.
- For **electricity**, they pull from `Electricity Fuel Cost per Unit Energy by Vehicle and Cargo Type[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]`, which mixes private vs. public charging costs (see §2).

The `Transportation - Cash Flow` sketch shows the unsubscripted versions removed and the new versions added. Downstream cash flow calculations now reference the subscripted version.

**Why this matters per CLAUDE.md.** This is technically a subscript-range *expansion of a referenced variable*, not a change to a subscript-range definition. But docs that walk through the fuel cost calculation prose-by-prose will need updating; the conceptual change is that **electricity cost is no longer a single price for all vehicles** — it depends on the public/private charging mix for each vehicle/cargo combination, which in 4.0.5 is BEV-share-dependent for passenger LDVs.

**Docs affected.**
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — "Calculating Cargo Distance Transported" subsection (image `FuelCostPerCargoDist`) describes the fuel-cost-per-unit-cargo-distance calc. Should mention electricity-cost-by-vehicle handling.
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — "Calculating NPV of Lifetime Vehicle Cost" → "NPV of Lifetime Annual Expenditures" subsection (image `FuelCostPerCargDist`). Same.
- `transportation-sector-cash.md` — "Change in Fuel Spending" subsection (image `Fuels`). The prose says "Transportation fuel use by fuel type is multiplied by the fuel cost per unit energy" — should add the vehicle/cargo dimension.

## 7. Vehicle stock calibration improvements 🟢

**What changed.** 4.0.5 introduces calibration helpers and supporting share variables:
- `Last Year Vehicles[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type, Vehicle Technology]` — already existed in 4.0.4 (unchanged). Listed here for clarity because it now has a new derived consumer.
- `Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type, Vehicle Technology]` — **new** share-of-stock-by-technology variable. Drives the new shadow-cost decay (§1) and the home-charger-installation share (§2).
- `BAU Last Year Percent Vehicle Stock by Vehicle Technology[...]` — BAU mirror, also new.
- `ANVCV Additional New Vehicles Calibration Variable[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]` — **new** input data. Read from `InputData/trans/ANVCV/ANVCV-{psgr,frgt}.csv`. Used as an *additive correction* in the `New Vehicles Demanded` calculation: `New Vehicles Demanded = round(distance / annual_distance_per_vehicle) + ANVCV[Vehicle Type, Cargo Type]`. Per the input file's About tab: "This variable can adjust sales of new vehicles in historical years to aid in calibration. The sum of each row should be zero." So it shifts vehicle sales between years rather than adding net new vehicles.
- `Start Year Vehicles Share by Technology` — share form of `SYVbT Start Year Vehicles by Technology`. Used inside `Retiring Vehicles` together with ANVCV to keep retirement accounting consistent with calibration corrections.

The `Vehicles`, `New Vehicles`, `Retiring Vehicles`, and `Last Year Vehicles` core level equations are **byte-identical to 4.0.4** (verified). The new layer is auxiliary.

Additionally: 4.0.5 changes the **vehicle-choice logit** to operate on `New Vehicle Cost per Mile` instead of `NPV of Lifetime Vehicle Cost`. The logit equation in 4.0.5 is `share ∝ shareweight × exp(cost_per_mile × unit_correction × logit_exponent)` (no `MAX(1, ...)` floor). Substantive change to vehicle technology allocation — moves the comparison onto a per-mile basis so vehicles with different annual usage / ownership periods are compared on a like-for-like basis. The `New Vehicle Cost per Mile = NPV / (annual_distance × loading × ownership_period / one_year)` definition (§8) supports this.

**Docs affected.**
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — "Tracking the Vehicle Fleet" subsection (image `TrackVehFleet`). The existing prose about the level variable manual-update mechanic is still correct. A **brief sentence** could note that 4.0.5 also tracks a one-year-lagged version of the level for use in shadow-cost decay calculations. Optional, depending on how granular staff wants the doc.
- `transportation-sector-main.md` — "Calculating New Cargo Distance Transported" subsection (image `NewCargoDist`). The new `ANVCV` calibration variable likely feeds here — **needs equation trace in Phase 3** to confirm before adding a paragraph.

## 1. Unified RPS / CES framework (`Elec Portfolio Std`) 🟢

**What changed.** 4.0.4 used `RPS` (Renewable Portfolio Standard) as the model's only clean-electricity standard mechanism. 4.0.5 generalizes this into a single `Elec Portfolio Std` framework subscripted by `Clean Elec Requirement: {RPS, CES}`, allowing a model run to enforce a renewables-only RPS, a broader Clean Energy Standard, or both simultaneously.

**Concrete renames** (representative, not exhaustive — these patterns repeat across ~80 variables):

| 4.0.4 name | 4.0.5 name |
| --- | --- |
| `Actual RPS Qualifying Electricity Generation` | `Actual Elec Portfolio Std Qualifying Electricity Generation[Clean Elec Requirement]` |
| `Available Capacity After RPS Dispatch[Electricity Timeslice, Hour, Electricity Source]` | `Available Capacity After Elec Portfolio Std Dispatch[Electricity Timeslice, Hour, Electricity Source]` |
| `BAU Electricity Dispatch Required to Satisfy RPS[Electricity Timeslice, Hour]` | `BAU Electricity Dispatch Required to Satisfy Elec Portfolio Stds[Electricity Timeslice, Hour, Clean Elec Requirement]` |
| `BAU Weighted Average RPS Percentage by Year` | `BAU Weighted Average Elec Portfolio Percentage by Year[Clean Elec Requirement]` |

**New input data:**
- `BRPSDbS BAU CES Percentages by Subregion[Subregion, Future Year]` — read from `InputData/elec/BRPSDbS/BRPSDbS-CES-percentages.csv`. Companion to the existing `BAU RPS Percentages by Subregion`.
- `Boolean Exclude Elec Exports from Elec Portfolio Stds[Clean Elec Requirement]` — control setting (`InputData/ctrl-settings/BEEEfEPS/`), `Dmnl [0,1,1]`, separately for RPS and CES. Lets users decide whether exported electricity counts toward the standard.

**Twin RPS/CES optimization machinery:** `RPS Optimization Step Size`, `RPS Credit Price by Optimization Pass`, `RPS Steps in {Negative,Positive,Same} Direction`, `Optimized RPS Credit Price`, `Total New and Retrofit Capacity Desired due to Cost Effectiveness by RPS Optimization Pass`, plus a parallel `... by CES Optimization Pass` family. The RPS and CES targets are pursued in separate optimization passes; new capacity additions for cost-effectiveness are computed for each independently and combined.

**Where it lives.** `Electricity Supply - Main` and BAU mirror.

## 3. Reliability machinery refactor — two-step → dispatchable-only 🟢

**What changed (corrected after Dan's review).** 4.0.4 ran a **two-step** reliability mechanism: a first pass added dispatchable-only resources for seasonal reliability, then a second "residual" pass could add a broader set of resources (the `Reliability Electricity Source within Electricity Source` subset, which extended past dispatchable-only) to fill any remaining reliability gap. 4.0.5 **removes the residual second pass entirely** and restricts reliability additions to dispatchable resources only. The remaining dispatchable step contains two parallel sub-passes (clean dispatchable + general dispatchable) sized via a new "Binding Peak Hour" identification.

**Smoking-gun equation diff.** In `New Generation Capacity Excluding Retrofit Capacity`:

- 4.0.4: `= New Generation Capacity after Dispatchable Only Reliability Additions + New Residual Capacity Desired for Seasonal Reliability`
- 4.0.5: `= New Generation Capacity after Dispatchable Only Reliability Additions` (the residual term is gone)

Same pattern in the supplementary `Total New Capacity for Reliability` metric: 4.0.4 sums `New Dispatchable Only Capacity Desired + New Residual Capacity Desired`; 4.0.5 has only the dispatchable-only term. The grid-battery additions chain (`New Grid Battery Capacity`) similarly drops the `+ SUM(New Grid Battery Capacity Deployed for Seasonal Residual Reliability by Optimization Pass)` term.

**Concrete additions and removals:**

- Removed: `New Residual Capacity Desired for Seasonal Reliability`, `Profitable Capacity for Seasonal Residual Reliability Additions`, `New Capacity Desired for Seasonal Residual Reliability by Optimization Pass`, `Capacity Market Optimization Step Size for Seasonal Residual Reliability`, `Capacity Market Price for Seasonal Residual Reliability` and its by-optimization-pass / adjusted-for-availability variants, `Amortized Capacity Market Costs for Seasonal Residual Reliability`, `Annual Capacity Market Costs for Seasonal Residual Reliability`, plus ~30 more variables in the residual family. ("Seasonal Residual Reliability" goes from 176 occurrences in 4.0.4 to zero in 4.0.5.)
- Removed: `New Grid Battery Capacity Deployed for Seasonal Residual Reliability by Optimization Pass` from the grid-battery additions chain — grid batteries no longer get a residual-reliability addition either.
- Added: `Binding Peak Hour for Reliability Additions[peak day electricity timeslice, Hour]` — a 0/1 flag that marks the (timeslice, hour) combination with the largest unmet net demand.
- Added: `Binding Peak Hour for Clean Dispatchable Reliability[peak day electricity timeslice, Hour]` — same idea for the CES-qualifying clean-firm reliability sub-pass.
- Added: `Net Electricity Demand Relative to Available Supply by Hour before Reliability Additions`, `Net Electricity Clean Firm Demand Relative to Available Clean Firm Supply by Hour before Additions`, `Net Electricity Demand Relative to Available Supply in Binding Hour by Optimization Pass`.
- Added: `Maximum Potential Binding Hour Electricity Output by Hour for Dispatchable Reliability`, `Maximum Potential Binding Hour Peak Seasonal Day Electricity Output by Optimization Pass for Dispatchable Reliability under CES`.

**Behavior implication.** In 4.0.4, the residual pass could in principle add resource types beyond strictly dispatchable. In 4.0.5, those resource types can no longer enter the fleet through the reliability mechanism — they can only enter via the cost-effectiveness, portfolio-standard, and policy-mandate paths. This better matches real-world capacity planning, where reliability is provided by firm/dispatchable resources.

**Where it lives.** `Electricity Supply - Main` and BAU mirror; cash-flow capacity-market accounting.

**Docs affected.**
- `electricity-sector-main.md`, "Reliability Additions" subsection — full rewrite (Phase 3) drops the two-step framing and the "summer-vs-winter-peak" example (which justified the old two-step structure that no longer exists) and describes the dispatchable-only mechanism with two parallel sub-passes.

## 5. Battery dispatch refactor (cost-priority allocation) 🟢

**What changed.** 4.0.5 reworks how hybrid battery storage decides when to charge and discharge. The new mechanism uses Vensim's `ALLOCATE AVAILABLE` function to distribute charge/discharge across (timeslice, hour) based on the previous five-year average marginal dispatch cost.

**New variables:**
- `Battery Charge Parameters[Hybrid Electricity Source, Electricity Timeslice, Hour, ptype/ppriority/pwidth/pextra]` — `ALLOCATE AVAILABLE` parameter set. Priority = `−Last Year Five Year Average Marginal Dispatch Cost by Hour` (charge when prices are low).

**Scope note:** the `Hybrid Electricity Source` subscript in these variable names tells me this refactor is specifically for **hybrid** storage (paired with renewables, e.g., solar+battery). ## D5. Electricity cost-effectiveness additions — per-pass RPS/CES achievability + hybrid prioritization 🟡

This is the densest part of the delta (~30 of the +43 Main variables, plus BAU twins) and it **builds directly on the unified RPS/CES framework prose written in the Apr-30 electricity round** — so Phase 3 here means carefully extending recently-written sections, not writing from scratch. Flagged 🟡 because the framing/intent deserves staff confirmation before drafting.

Observed structure from the equations:
- Cost-effectiveness capacity additions are dual-tracked by `[Electricity Source, Clean Elec Requirement]` (the RPS/CES axis), e.g. `Max New Capacity Desired due to Cost Effectivness for RPS and CES`, `Max Profitable Capacity for Cost Effectiveness Additions for RPS and CES`, `Max Incremental Revenue for Cost Effectiveness Additions for RPS and CES`, `Max Retrofitted Capacity Desired…for RPS and CES`, and CCUS-retrofit variants.
- **Per-optimization-pass achievability gating (traced 2026-05-29).** `Max Achievable Annual RPS this Year` (and CES) = qualifying-output-limited share of qualifying demand, summed over timeslices/hours. `Max Achievable RPS Hit by Optimization Pass[pass]` = `1` if that pass's `Expected Achievable Annual RPS` equals `Max Achievable Annual RPS this Year` **and** that max is `<= Elec Portfolio Percentage to Seek This Year[RPS]` (i.e. the target is physically unachievable), else `9999`; `[last pass]` is hardcoded to `9999`. `Final RPS Pass Gate` = `1` iff `VMIN` over passes = 1 (≥1 pass hit). `Final RPS Pass Value` = the credit price at the hitting pass (`VMIN(hit-flag × RPS Credit Price by Optimization Pass[pass])`). **Consumer:** `RPS Credit Price by Optimization Pass[last pass]` — when the gate = 1, the last-pass credit price is set to `Final RPS Pass Value` instead of the normal up/down step search. **Net effect:** when the portfolio target cannot be physically exceeded, the optimizer locks the final credit price to the price that achieved maximum feasible buildout, rather than stepping it up toward the Alternative Compliance Payment cap. (CES twins identical.)
- **Explicit hybrid-power-plant prioritization stage (traced 2026-05-29).** A hybrid source (e.g. solar+storage) can be desired both as a "hybrid power plant" (storage role) and as a regular generation source. `…Before Hybrid Prioritization` variables compute each role's desired capacity independently; the post-prioritization `Max New Capacity Desired due to Cost Effectivness for RPS and CES[Hybrid Electricity Source]` and its `…for Hybrid Power Plants…` companion then **split the shared `Max Capacity Buildable after Mandated and Required Clean Dispatchable Additions` proportionally between the two roles via `ZIDZ`** when their sum would exceed what's buildable (full desired amounts otherwise). Non-hybrid sources get `0` in the hybrid branch (`:EXCEPT: [Hybrid Electricity Source, Clean Elec Requirement]`). It is a contention resolver, not a new build pathway. New supporting vars: `Marginal Capacity Factor by Hour for Hybrid Plants`, `Marginal Capacity Factor x Hours per Year for Hybrid Power Plants`, `Expected RPS/CES Qualifying Hourly Output from New Cost Effective Hybrid Plants`, `Max RPS and CES Revenue at Marginal Capacity Factors (for Hybrid Power Plants)`.
- New one-step-lag inputs feeding the above: `Last Year Electricity Generation by Type`, `Last Year Hourly Bid Electricity Capacity Factors for Reliability by Plant Type` (+ BAU twins).

**Removed (refactored away) 🟢:** the old "seed a plant type that currently has zero capacity" mechanism — `Hypothetical Annual Costs / Annual Revenue / Net Revenue for Plant Types with No Capacity (per MW)`, `Last Year Hypothetical Net Revenue for Plant Types with No Capacity per MW`, `Last Year Total Five Year Average Net Energy Market Revenue per MW`.
