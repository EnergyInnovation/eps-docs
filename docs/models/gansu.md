The Gansu Energy Policy Simulator (EPS) is a free and open-source computer model created by [Energy Innovation LLC (EI)](https://energyinnovation.org/) and the [Institute for Global Decarbonization Progress (iGDP)](http://www.igdp.cn/). It is adapted from software originally created by EI.

<p><a href="https://energypolicy.solutions/simulator/gansu/en" class="btn">Run the simulator in your browser</a></p>
